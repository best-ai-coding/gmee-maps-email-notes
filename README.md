# Field notes: pulling emails and local leads from Google Maps

Working notes for B2B outreach teams who need business contact rows from public Google Maps search results (name, email when available, phone, website, address, socials).

## Why this workflow

Cold outreach and agency prospecting often start with a niche + city search on Maps. Copy-pasting each card is slow; a structured export into CSV/XLSX/JSON is easier to drop into a CRM.

## Practical steps

1. Search Google Maps for a niche and location (e.g. "HVAC contractors in Austin").
2. Capture publicly shown fields: business name, email (when detectable), phone, website, address, category, rating, review count, Place ID / CID, lat/long, and social profile links.
3. Auto-paginate through result pages and dedupe before export.
4. Export CSV for CRM imports, XLSX for client handoff, or JSON for tooling.
