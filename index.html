Field notes: emails and local business leads from Google Maps

Notes for sales and agency teams who build outreach lists from public Google Maps results instead of copy-pasting every listing card.

Short workflow

  1. Run a Maps search for niche + city.
  2. Collect name, email when shown/detectable, phone, website, address, category, rating, reviews, Place ID/CID, coordinates, and social links.
  3. Paginate results, remove duplicates, export CSV / XLSX / JSON.

Tool I use: Google Maps Email Extractor — free 200-lead trial; see pricing and add to Chrome.
